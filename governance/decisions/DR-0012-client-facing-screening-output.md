# DR-0012: Client-facing screening output — amend BOUNDARIES.md to permit it

- **Status:** proposed — awaiting owner approval and the open Research Gate
- **Date:** 2026-08-03
- **Decider:** owner
- **Related:** `architecture/property-intelligence-v0.1.md`, charter §"GIS /
  property intelligence boundary", BOUNDARIES.md, DR-0004, DR-0005 (research
  gate), DR-0007, DR-0011 (proposed), RP-0003 §4

## Context

Owner intent of 2026-08-03: a visitor enters an address, immediately sees what
they may build, and receives a commercial offer by email.

As written today, the platform forbids all three parts of that sentence:

1. **Charter** — "Any lead score is internal prioritization only and must never
   be represented to a client as feasibility, entitlement, or approval." GIS
   output is currently an *internal* artifact. Showing it to a visitor is out
   of bounds regardless of how it is worded.
2. **BOUNDARIES.md** — AI may not "promise price or schedule" and may not "make
   permit / code / zoning / buildability / legal conclusions." RP-0003 §4
   already flagged that a competitor's cost-range output "our boundaries do not
   currently allow."
3. **BOUNDARIES.md** — AI may not "send client-facing messages." Emailing the
   report to the visitor is a client-facing message.

BOUNDARIES.md may be changed only by an owner-adopted decision record. This is
that record. It does not weaken the boundary; it draws a narrower one that the
owner's intent fits inside.

## Decision (proposed)

Amend the platform boundary along three seams, each conditioned on a mechanism
that removes the discretion the original prohibition was protecting against.

### 1. Screening output may face the client — as a sourced quotation

Permitted: publishing to a visitor the Layer 1 and Layer 2 output defined in
`property-intelligence-v0.1.md`, provided that

- every Layer 1 field carries its source, layer, and read date;
- every Layer 2 statement is produced by the dated, owner-verified rules table
  and phrased categorically, never as a determination about their parcel;
- the required wording "Requires official source verification" is present;
- the blind-spot disclosure (§6 of the design) is present, not collapsed into
  fine print;
- an absent, stale, or contradictory source yields a refusal, never a gap-fill.

Still prohibited, unchanged: any statement that a specific project is
approvable, permittable, or buildable; any lead score shown to a client.

Rationale: the original prohibition protects against the platform *deciding*
something it has no standing to decide. Quoting a public record and evaluating
a published statutory floor decides nothing.

### 2. An indicative range may be shown — only as a lookup in a signed document

Permitted: displaying a price range retrieved from the owner-approved price
book, with its validity date shown, and omitted entirely once expired.

Still prohibited, unchanged: any price computed, estimated, adjusted,
negotiated, or generated by a model; any schedule commitment; any bid or
contract without owner signature.

Rationale: the boundary exists so no price reaches a client that the owner did
not approve. A lookup in a document the owner signed satisfies that fully. The
distinction is not cosmetic — it is the difference between the platform
speaking and the owner speaking through the platform.

### 3. The report may be emailed — as an approved template, deterministically

Permitted: delivering the report to the address the visitor supplied, using a
templated message the owner approved in advance, containing no generated prose.

Still prohibited, unchanged: any model-authored client-facing text; any
follow-up, sequence, campaign, or second message (each remains `external_io`
requiring its own approved packet); any message to anyone the visitor did not
identify as themselves.

Rationale: the prohibition targets a model deciding what to say to a client.
A fixed template the owner wrote is the owner saying it.

## Conditions of adoption

This record may not be marked adopted until:

- the Research Gate items in `property-intelligence-v0.1.md` §8 are closed,
  particularly the statute citations, source terms of use, and the legal review
  of the report as a document;
- DR-0011 is adopted with a destination chosen;
- DR-0008 is adopted;
- the first jurisdiction's coverage matrix is filled in with verified answers.

Adopting this record before those close would put a client-facing legal-adjacent
document into the market on unverified inputs, which is the specific failure the
Research Gate (DR-0005) exists to prevent.

## What this record does NOT decide

- Any vendor, provider, geocoder, or email service.
- The contents of the price book or the rules table — both are owner inputs.
- Consent, CCPA notice, or tracking behavior (DR-0007 territory).
- Whether the report ever flows through the evidence core.
- Any Tier 2 or Tier 3 funnel behavior beyond the single report email.

## Consequences

- BOUNDARIES.md gains three narrowed clauses rather than three deletions; the
  edit ships with the implementing task packet, not before.
- The price book and the rules table become owner-maintained artifacts with real
  operational weight — expiry is enforced by the system, not by memory.
- Screening honesty becomes a product feature: visible refusals and stated blind
  spots are the argument, and softening either one breaks the rationale that
  justified this amendment.

## Revisit trigger

Any request to show a visitor a number that does not come from a public record,
the dated rules table, or the signed price book. That request means this
amendment is being stretched past what justified it.
