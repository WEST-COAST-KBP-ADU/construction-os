## Decision and rationale

The old doctrine made the living operating state, rather than a house, the Hero
subject and treated Concept Studio as strictly property-agnostic. The adopted
doctrine allows one premium small, owned, concept-only ADU in a credible
California property/construction scene to become the emotional and causal portal
into the same editable Studio design idea. One finite camera transition may carry
that causal move. Reduced-motion and no-motion preserve equivalent start and end
meaning.

This retains the first-five-second reading of West Coast KBP as a premium ADU and
general-construction business. The house is neither an orderable product nor a
catalogue, completed-project claim, feasibility statement, or property
conclusion. KBP OS remains under the surface through sources, assumptions,
unknowns, constraints, human review, and the safest bounded next action.

The change resolves the mismatch between the Owner's selected house-portal
direction and the old no-house visual target while preserving the existing
authority and claim boundaries.

## Visual adoption versus GIS separation

The house-centered visual doctrine is adopted product direction. Real-address,
parcel/GIS, and geometric-fit capability is separately `DIRECTION · NOT OPENED`.

separate Owner-adopted amendment to higher-authority
`governance/BOUNDARIES.md`, and a separately bounded implementation packet.

`Requires official source verification.`
