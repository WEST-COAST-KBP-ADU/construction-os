## Key findings (as claimed by the document)

- 12-competitor UX teardown. Top tier (Abodu, LADU, Vision ADU, SnapADU) run
  full sales-funnel sites: interactive property tool first, education nurture,
  low-friction calendar booking. The defining trend: **address-first /
  tool-first intake replacing contact forms** (LADU "Check My Property",
  Abodu FutureLot, GBG CostHammer).
- Universal homepage patterns (hero, numbered process, gallery, named
  testimonials, CSLB license, service areas, financing) with frequency table.
- Market-wide gaps: no real client portal anywhere, no Sacramento permit
  timeline data, no ROI calculator done well, no bilingual NorCal content, no
  referral programs, weak warranty specifics.
- Full design blueprint: sitemap (Services/Process/Portfolio/Floor Plans/
  Pricing/Why Us/Resources/Contact/Client Portal), 15-section homepage order,
  navigation, visual style (deep forest green + Sacramento gold, DM Sans/Inter,
  real photos only), 3-tier lead funnel (anonymous tool → email gate →
  calendar), 10-module client portal, 8 Sacramento-specific differentiators
  (permit timeline map, price lock, **bilingual EN+RU portal**, ROI calculator,
  comparison table, grant hub, referral program, city guide series).

## Alignment with our platform

The client portal blueprint is effectively a public projection of our own
object-control model (scope, state, milestones, approvals, evidence, next
action — charter). The 3-tier funnel maps cleanly onto candidate → qualified →
owner review lead states. This is the strongest external validation yet that
the Construction OS concept is the differentiator.

## Conflicts / owner decisions needed

1. **Cost display.** Blueprint recommends interactive cost estimator with
   ranges and "starting from" pricing; our boundaries currently forbid public
   cost commitments. Needs a cost-content policy decision (educational ranges
   with disclaimers vs. no numbers).
2. **Timeline display.** Week-numbered timelines are recommended; our
   boundaries forbid schedule promises. Same policy decision — ranges with
   disclaimers vs. none. (Nonna Homes died from over-promised timelines; the
   document itself flags this.)
3. **Russian-language portal pages** are a differentiation idea; DR-0003
   disables Russian only for the public phone voice, so site pages are
   permitted — but it is a positioning decision for the owner.
4. **Client portal, payments, change orders** involve production PII and
   financial workflows — far beyond current authorizations; portal phase
   requires its own decision records.
