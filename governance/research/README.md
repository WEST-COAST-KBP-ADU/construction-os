# Research Packets

Outputs of the Research Gate (DR-0005). Research has **zero authority**: it is
not SourceTrue and decides nothing. A packet becomes actionable only when
synthesized into an owner-adopted decision record.

Every unverified pricing, latency, model, GA, benchmark, or compliance claim
must carry the wording: "Requires official source verification."
