## What the product is

**KBP OS — the lead generation and process management platform for ADU and
General Construction.** Explicitly open to any General Construction project,
**residential and commercial**. It runs the construction business: reception
of inquiries, consented customer memory, project and lead lifecycle, evidence
of work — and it is itself the first public deployment of the Deedseal
platform (Product 1's first client, "Powered by Deedseal" per the adopted
cross-repository boundary decisions).

## Positioning to fix on every public surface (through gates)

- Category: operating system for a construction business — not a website, not
  a CRM subscription.
- Scope: ADU as the sharpened wedge; General Construction — residential and
  commercial — as the open field. The copy must say both.
- Proof style: what the system does is demonstrated by the running system,
  never asserted. Uncertain GIS/jurisdiction/feasibility output always carries
  `Requires official source verification.`
